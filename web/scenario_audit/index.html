Instructions · 标注说明

Each item shows a claim and a bundle of retrieved pages. Every page lists which other page it cites (cites → …). Your job is to verify the citation structure itself, not whether the claim is true.
每道题给出一个断言和一组检索到的网页；每个页面都标明它引用了哪个页面（cites → …）。你的任务是核验引用结构本身，而不是判断断言是否为真。

  • Follow the cites links. Work out where each page's information ultimately comes from.
    顺着 cites 链接，追出每个页面的信息最终来自哪里。
  • Judge only from the shown pages. No web search, no outside knowledge, no AI tools.
    只依据展示的页面判断：不搜索、不用外部知识、不用 AI 工具。
  • The entities are fictional by design (placeholder .example domains). That is expected and is not what you are auditing.
    实体是刻意虚构的（.example 占位域名）。这是设计使然，不是你要审计的问题。
  • Each item shows the MAIN bundle (blue edge) and usually a collapsed SOLID bundle (purple edge), a separate evidence set for the same claim. Questions 1–5 are about the MAIN bundle only; Question 6 is about the SOLID bundle only. For Q6 you judge whether the SOLID set contains at least two independent primary sources (first-hand records that support the value without citing each other).
    每题包含主证据集（蓝边）和通常折叠着的紫边 SOLID 证据集（同一断言的另一组材料）。第 1–5 题只看主证据集；第 6 题只看 SOLID 集：判断其中是否有至少两个互不引用的一手来源分别支持该数值。
  • A scenario may or may not satisfy each property — answer what you actually see.
    每条性质可能满足也可能不满足，按你实际看到的回答。
  • Chinese translations of the materials are machine-generated reading aids; the English text is authoritative — check the English wording when judging how weak or confident a source sounds.
    材料的中文翻译由机器生成、仅供辅助阅读；以英文原文为准——尤其在判断来源语气强弱（是猜测还是确证）时，请核对英文措辞。右上角可开关中文。

Time: ~2–4 minutes per item. Your answers auto-save; you can leave and resume any time. Participation is voluntary and you may stop at any point.
每题约 2–4 分钟；答案自动保存，可随时离开、下次继续；参与自愿，可随时退出。
